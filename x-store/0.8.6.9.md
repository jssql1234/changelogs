This release updates order processing for Shopify, reduces the consumer worker count, and preserves platform-provided order dates and time zones during posting.

<!-- truncate -->

## Updates

- **Order**:
  - Reduced the consumer worker count
  - Removed time-zone conversion during order posting so that posted orders use the date and time zone provided by the platform
  - Removed handling for Shopify orders with the To Ship and Shipped statuses (assumption of all Shopify orders are Self Deliver)
